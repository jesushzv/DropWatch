Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 August 2026

DropWatch is an early-stage product, operated by an independent founder. It has not launched yet. Today this site does exactly two things: it takes an email address if you choose to give one, and it measures whether people find the idea interesting. This page explains precisely what that involves.

The short version. We collect your email only if you type it in. We never sell it, never rent it, and never send newsletters to it. We use analytics and an advertising pixel to count visits and signups — neither one ever receives your email address. Ask us and we will delete everything we hold about you.

What we collect

WhatWhenWhy
Your email address, and which part of the page you signed up from (including a pricing tier name, if you clicked one) Only when you submit the signup form To email you once when early access opens, with your invite and your 3 free months of Pro
Product analytics: pages viewed, buttons clicked, signup submissions, referring site and campaign tags in the link you arrived through, browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address Every visit To count how many people visit and what share of them sign up — the entire point of this page right now
Advertising measurement: a page-view signal, and a "lead" signal when a signup happens Every visit, if you arrived while our ads are running To tell whether our ads reached anyone who cared. Your email address is never included
Standard server logs: IP address, browser user-agent, time of request Every visit Automatically recorded by our host to serve the page and block abuse

We do not record your screen or your mouse movements, we do not ask for a password or payment details, and there is nothing to log into. We do not knowingly collect anything from children under 13.

Who else touches this data

We use a small number of outside services. Each one only receives what it needs:

  • Supabase — hosts the database holding signup emails.
  • PostHog — product analytics. Receives page views and click events. Never receives your email address.
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — advertising measurement, via the Meta Pixel. Receives page-view and lead signals and may set cookies and link them to a Facebook or Instagram account it already recognises. Never receives your email address. You can limit this in your Meta account settings or with browser tracking protection.
  • Vercel — hosts and serves this website.
  • Google Fonts — serves the two typefaces on this page. Loading them sends your IP address to Google.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not share your email address with anyone. If we ever add a new service that handles your data, this page is updated in the same release.

Cookies and similar technology

Our analytics and the Meta Pixel set cookies in your browser to avoid counting the same visitor twice and to measure ad performance. Nothing on this site requires cookies to function — block them and the page still works normally. Your browser's cookie settings, private-browsing mode, or a tracking blocker will all limit or remove this collection.

How long we keep it

Signup emails are kept until DropWatch launches and you have received your invite, or until you ask us to delete yours — whichever comes first. If DropWatch never launches, we delete the entire signup list and will tell you we have done so. Analytics and advertising data are retained on our providers' standard schedules and are not tied to your email address.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. If you are in the UK, EU, or a US state with its own privacy law, you additionally have the right to object to processing and to complain to your local data protection authority. We do not charge for any of this and we will not ask you to justify the request.

Email privacy@usedropwatch.com and we will action it within 30 days. Deleting your signup means removing your address from the database entirely — it does not survive somewhere else.

Changes to this policy

If what we collect changes, we change this page and update the date at the top. Because DropWatch is still being built, that is likely to happen at least once before launch.

Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or anything else about privacy: privacy@usedropwatch.com.
